# Escalation policy

## When to escalate

Escalate to the on-call engineer when a customer-facing system has been down for more than 15 minutes,
or when a customer reports a data or security issue.

## How to escalate

Post in the #support-urgent channel with the ticket number and a one-line summary. The on-call engineer
acknowledges within 10 minutes during business hours.
