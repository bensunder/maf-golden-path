# Refund policy

## Who can refund

Support agents may refund up to $50 per order on their own. Refunds over $50 need approval from a
refunds lead before the money is returned. A refund can never exceed the order total.

## Reasons we accept

Damaged or dented packaging, late delivery (more than 5 business days past the ETA), a wrong item, or a
change of mind within 30 days of delivery.

## Timing

Approved refunds reach the customer's original payment method within 5 business days.
